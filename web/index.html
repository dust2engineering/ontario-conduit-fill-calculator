Basis and scope

CEC Rule 12-910 with Table 8 fill limits (1 conductor 53%, 2 conductors 31%, 3 or more 40%) and RW90 conductor areas from Table 6A. Conduit areas are the CEC Table 9-series values, published at the 40% level; totals for the 53%/31% cases are derived from them. EMT is limited to the publicly verifiable trade sizes (21, 27, 41, 63 mm); rigid PVC is the full range. Short nipples up to 600 mm may be filled to 60%.
